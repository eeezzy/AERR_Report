Conclusion

The project shows how classical navigation, exploration, and geometric perception can be composed with industrial manipulator tooling to prototype emergency-style logistics. The hardest integration work was reliable camera–LiDAR fusion on the Turtlebot and consistent multi-robot transforms across domains.

Challenges (from deployment)

  • Turtlebot: ROS namespacing for topics, Nav2 inflation and footprint tuning, irregular lab geometry, and fusion stability.
  • UR7e: ArUco detection robustness, blocks not landing cleanly in the box, and error accumulation over long sequences.

Improvements and extensions

  • Improvements: stronger PID on the UR7e, handling multiple blocks per cycle, and richer cone inspection (e.g., orbiting cones for color confirmation).
  • Extensions: tactile or audio cues (buzzer) for mission state, additional shapes and colors, playful knock-over tasks, and fully autonomous last-mile delivery directly at the hazard pose.
