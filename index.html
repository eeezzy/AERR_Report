EECS/MecEng C106A

Autonomous Emergency Response Robot

A dual-robot ROS 2 system that combines a Turtlebot3 and a UR7e manipulator to demonstrate an end-to-end emergency-response workflow.

Team #9  |  Pranav Bhatt, I Gy Chen, Jaemin Jang, Christian Jimenez

Explore Project

Introduction

The original goal was to demonstrate an end-to-end emergency response system at a small scale, integrating the major themes of C106A (Control, Dynamics, and Perception) in a single project.

By coordinating two robots instead of one, our team could tackle a richer problem: how to use both robots together to complete a delivery task suited to emergency response. The Turtlebot surveys the space, builds a map, and locates colored cones while the UR7e arm delivers the correct colored block to a staging mount so the mobile robot can complete mitigation at the cone.
