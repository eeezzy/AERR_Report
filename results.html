Results

The turtlebot successfully maps the enclosed environment and detects all three different colored hazards. It then docks at its starting point and the UR7e accurately loads the blocks into the storage basin, where an ArUco tag provides a guide for delivery. Finally, the turtlebot delivers the correct colored block to a person to neutralize the hazard of similar color consecutively while avoiding the hazards. The demo video shows our successful result of this sequence in action. We were able to demonstrate the whole pipeline as shown below as well as the point of view of the turtlebot entirely autonomously.
