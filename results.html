Results

The integrated stack runs from a single launch entry point, with variants for simulation versus hardware and manual versus automatic mapping controlled via command-line arguments. The Turtlebot performs frontier-based exploration with Nav2 and the SLAM Toolbox, fuses camera color cues with LiDAR geometry to publish stable hazard tracks, and hands off mission phases through a dedicated Mission Manager finite state machine.

The UR7e side executes perception (depth-based colored blocks, ArUco on the mount), motion planning with MoveIt and inverse kinematics, and gripper actuation, signaling completion so the Turtlebot can return to the dock, receive the block, and navigate to a confirmed hazard.
